# Privacy

Face embeddings are biometric data. Use this application only with photos you own or for which you have informed consent. It is designed for local/self-hosted use; hosted LLM/VLM configuration sends image content to that provider. Do not expose it publicly without authentication or use it for surveillance, stranger identification, or decisions about people.

The People screen supports deleting a person, which removes their face records and vector entries. Review applicable privacy law, including consent and erasure obligations, before deployment.
